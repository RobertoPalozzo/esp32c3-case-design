Case design for ESP32C3 Zero

Documenting the design and prototyping process of a 3D printed case for the ESP32C3 Zero microcontroller.

Scopo del sito

This site documents the complete design and development process for a 3D-printed protective case for the ESP32C3 Zero microcontroller. The case is designed to protect the circuit during normal classroom use while still allowing access to the USB port, GPIO pins, and the BOOT and RST buttons.

Through two prototyping phases, the improvements made to each version are demonstrated until the final design, documenting problems encountered, solutions adopted, and test results.

ESP32C3 Zero Case Project

Documentation of the design and prototyping process for a 3D-printed case for the ESP32C3 Zero microcontroller.

Purpose of This Site

This site documents the complete design and development process of a 3D-printed protective case for the ESP32C3 Zero microcontroller. The case was designed to protect the circuit during normal classroom use, while still allowing access to the USB port, GPIO pins, and the BOOT and RST buttons.

Through two prototyping phases, the improvements made to each version are shown leading up to the final design, documenting the issues encountered, the solutions adopted, and the results of each test.
